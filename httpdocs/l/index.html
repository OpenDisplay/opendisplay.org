Device not showing up?
  1. Turn on Bluetooth and hold your phone close to the device.
  2. On Android, turn on Location too. Chrome needs it to find Bluetooth devices.
  3. The device advertises every second. If it still doesn’t appear, press its button once to wake it.
  4. Close other apps or tabs connected to it. It talks to one at a time.
  5. In your browser’s list, pick the one starting with OD.
